# Privacy Policy – RIFFS (CS Elective 2 Final Exam)

RIFFS is a student project for CS Elective 2 at Ateneo de Davao University. It is a
demo store and does not sell real products or take payments.

## What we collect

- Your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google or enter it at sign-up.
- The orders you place in the demo store.

## How it is used

This information is used only to sign you in, show your account role (customer or
admin), and show your order history inside the app. It is stored in the project's
Supabase database.

## Sharing

Your information is not sold or shared with anyone. Only the project's developers and
the course instructor can see it, for grading.

## Deleting your data

Email aedraventura@addu.edu.ph to have your account and orders deleted.
